# Where seed anchors go wrong, and the copy test that fixes them (2026-10-23)

Every exact match of 21 letters or more between two sequences, checked letter by letter against every optimal
alignment of the pair (full gap-affine forward and backward DP): `anchors --brute <fa> <a> <b> <period>
[--prices x,o,e] [--anchors]`, over vntr-eval's regions with `experiments/anchors/brute_panel.py` and
`trace_panel.py` (426 pairs, 146 regions; three pairs per region, at most 60M cells).

## What a seed is worth inside a tandem array

On L014210 (period 27), CHM13 against GRCh38 (59 copies fewer): 22 of the 28 MUMs lie on no optimal alignment,
all of them in the array, 10 to 138 copies off; the right ones are the flanks'. A 381-letter MUM unique in both
(CHM13 against sequence 11) is 37 copies from its optimal partner. The optimum spreads the copy-number difference
along the whole array, its diagonal drifting with the copies' variants; the evidence is many short matches, not
one long one. Over the panel, 57% of the wrong MUMs in arrays are longer than the longest exact run an optimal
alignment has in their span (92% under affine prices): exact uniqueness inside an array is chance, not homology.

## The two bugs (every anchor off an optimum under affine prices, traced)

1. The repeat mask misses long-period duplications. TR101140 5/8: sequence 5 holds two diverged copies of a stretch
   of sequence 8, about 640 letters apart; no 12-mer recurs within the mask's 500 letters, so six MUMs in it pass as
   flanks, chained to the nearer, wrong copy (excess 86-88 each).
2. The split rule takes a window's length difference for one indel. The optimum spreads it over several copies'
   indels (TR724750: -165 as -42, -42 and -41; L011463: -54 as -30 and -24), or shifts a copy and back inside a
   window whose net difference is nil (L005411: +23 and -24 around a 223-letter MUM one copy off, excess 52;
   L003826: +34, -58).

The fix (`split_anchors(ambiguity=0.1)`, the default): an anchor whose letters match the other sequence on another
diagonal of its window with at most 10% mismatched, either way round, has a copy; a flank anchor with one is
dropped, a window passes over it for the next longest. At 3% and 5% L005411 stays (copies over 5% apart); 10% and
20% leave none, 20% at 6% fewer anchored letters. Under unit cost what remains are ties: a long deletion spreads
into many small ones at no cost (L013074 loses 4.9 kb), so an anchor on the window's diagonal costs 1 to 7.

## A corridor cannot be proved cheaply

Any alignment leaving a corridor costs at least the corridor's best to where it leaves, plus a gap letter per
diagonal to where it returns for good, plus the corridor's best from there. That bound is sound (every proof found
the optimum) but proves the corridor only at full width, 260-275% of the full DP's cells: once the flanks are
pinned the pairs cost little more than their length difference, and only the mismatches of a detour could rule it
out. Unproved stopping (three doublings agreeing) misses by 150 under affine on the panel at 80-90% of the cells,
so at VNTR sizes the full DP stays; corridors pay only where the needed width is small against the sequences.

## The graph mode (`-S -J`): correct anchors are not the point

The copy test leaves the quick set level overall (12 of 24 regions unchanged), better on L012256 (1.131 to 1.094)
and L013064, worse on L002143 (1.111 to 1.292). The seed audit (`PLEGMA_SEED_DUMP=<file> plegma -S ...`, then
`anchors --seed-audit <file>`) shows both builds' anchors exactly optimal against the consensus path at all 33
steps of L002143: the copy test removes right anchors (a 1633-letter match on the main diagonal has a copy one
period over) and the windows' graph alignment places those stretches worse for the MSA than the anchors did.
Anchoring each sequence on its exact optimal alignment to the path (every run of 21+ matched letters) is worse
still on the quick set's unit metric (hotspot VNTR 1.187 to 1.31-1.35); anchoring to the nearest sequence's path
instead of the consensus helps L002143 a little (1.292 to 1.258) and was dropped. Placing each sequence by its
pairwise optimum against one reference conflicts with the copies the others hold; `-A` (votes from many placed
sequences' optimal alignments, then refinement) is the mode that wins: hotspot VNTR 1.0547 / 1.1210 on the same 24
against 1.1410 / 1.1755 for the best `-S -J` and 1.0947 / 1.1291 for unit_aware.

## Why plegma's pair F1 is lower: the objective, not the search (hard set, six regions)

`experiments/hardset/hardset.py --diagnose` on the six cheapest of the 45 hard-set regions (medians; pair F1 is
pgrealign qc's, `sp` the MSA's summed cost under pgrealign's scores over the star's):

| MSA | pair F1 | co_f1 | sp |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| pgrealign star | 0.800 | 0.811 | 1 |
| `-A` (default) | 0.746 | 0.761 | 1.004 |
| `-A -U -W 6,2 -I 20,64,8 -V -2,2` | 0.774 | 0.794 | 0.933 |
| the same, 3 voters (PLEGMA_VOTERS=3) | 0.775 | 0.798 | 0.900 |
| the same, no refinement | 0.747 | 0.763 | 0.985 |
| the star refined by plegma (`--start star`, -W 6,2 open 8) | 0.756 | 0.765 | 0.917 |

75-83% of the wrong letters are whole copies shifted, 13-18% letters paired where the optimum gaps them; ties
(co_f1 - f1) are 0.01-0.02. Refining the star by the sum of pairs lowers its summed cost by 8% (TR_chr20_65831520:
the scored pairs from 1.45 times their optima to 1.02) and its F1 from 0.800 to 0.756: the sum of pairs spreads the
cost over every pair, pair F1 counts the pairs left exactly optimal, which the medoid star makes many of at a large
cost to a few. Which serves read mapping better is the question the chr20 arms answer.

## Genotyping on 387 chr20 regions: what the remaining errors are (2026-10-24)

`experiments/chr20/batch_call.py --sample 32` (each region's graph reduced to CHM13 and the 32 alleles the reads
support best, as production's vg haplotypes does), on the 370-381 regions every MSA completed; structural F1 from
vg-call-eval's truvari scoring, haplotype edits from `experiments/chr20/haps.py` (HG002's two truth haplotypes against
the two vg call's records spell; "least" is the truth against its nearest rows in the graph):

| MSA | structural F1 (381) | small F1 (387) | haplotype edits (381; least 30,730) |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| pgrealign star | 0.556 | 0.445 | 130,581 |
| plegma `-A` (the chr20 arm's) | 0.379 (370) | 0.417 | - |
| `-A -Q` | 0.572 | 0.481 | 155,410 |
| `-A -Q -I 20,64,8 -W 6,2` | **0.576** | **0.483** | 147,616 |
| `-A -Q`, centre tree at 5% (PLEGMA_TREE=0.05, WEIGHTED=1) | 0.538 | 0.478 | 195,588 |
| `-A -Q`, centre tree at 2% | 0.561 | 0.478 | 177,774 |
| `-A -Q`, tree in Prim's order (every allele a centre) | 0.452 | 0.466 | - |

- The graph almost always holds HG002's alleles (least 30,730 edits over 762 haplotypes); vg call's choices add
  100-165k. Most of that is in regions whose indel calls have GQ <= 3 (coin flips among similar copy numbers, a few
  reads each): genotyper noise every MSA shares. In confident regions (every indel call GQ > 10) `-A -Q`'s excess
  was 8,326 edits against the star's 3,761.
- Called haplotypes are often mosaics (no row within 10 edits; 49-64 regions per MSA): the ~60 small snarls of a
  long array are genotyped apart.
- `-A -Q`'s confident misses come from alleles that share an event but meet the reference at their own optima:
  TR_chr20_55609810, where 112 haplotypes carry DEL1348, a12 (a4 plus 404 letters) is D719+D176+D40 against CHM13,
  each such allele with its own breakpoints, and one snarl of near-identical traversals splits the reads. The star,
  each allele through the commonest, makes it D1348 + I404 and calls the deletion right.
- `on_tree` (each allele through its most similar heavy allele, the heavy ones heaviest first from the reference)
  makes those families consistent and fixes such regions (35425527: 1,860 edits to 51; 65768408: 1,819 to 374), but
  where the commonest allele is itself a patchwork of copy differences (TR_chr20_63572360: a5, 67 haplotypes, is four
  deletions of CHM13) its members inherit the patchwork and add copies back next to it (a10: I126 plus a5's four
  deletions, its own optimum D375 + D187), and those insertion-deletion pairs cost more than the families gain.
  Rejected as a default; kept behind PLEGMA_TREE for experiments.
- Parsimony (an allele joins through its centre only where that leaves it fewer insertions and deletions against
  CHM13 than its own alignment, else straight onto CHM13) keeps the families that gain and drops the patchwork: 0.513
  (5%) / 0.517 (2%), lower still, because the tree gave each allele's insertions columns of their own. With the runs
  inserted at one place aligned to each other as `-A -Q` aligns them (`on_tree` now hands its pairings to the same
  column builder), 0.5725 against `-A -Q`'s 0.5722 on 381 regions (small 0.481 both); 117 regions change, 28 better
  and 34 worse in haplotype edits, the totals swung by two coin-flip regions of very long alleles (TR_chr20_45868581,
  49643268). Merging the insertions at a place is what matters (0.51 to 0.57); the tree over that is neutral.
  `-A -Q` stays the default.
