- `-A -U` is the harness's progressive placement moved into plegma (`plegma/progressive.mojo`): the same within
  noise.
- The copy-level start (`plegma/units.mojo`) alone changes little; with the refinement told the period (bands
  reaching a copy, group moves splitting the rows by copy number) it is the best on every hotspot metric.
- Head to head, the final `-A` against unit_aware: better on both metrics in 54 regions, worse on both in 3
  (L011227, L014111, L011121; 13 before this work). Against the harness: better on both in 15, worse in 3.
- The regions traced: L013244 1.010 / 1.020 (unit_aware 1.013 / 1.025), L014210 1.042 / 1.067 (1.059 / 1.087).
- Time: 942 s wall over 8 jobs for the 149 (7,159 s summed; the slowest region 928 s).

Why copy-aware moves (`copy_shift.py`): where our MSA and unit_aware's differ, most letters face partners offset by
whole copies (L014210: 25% of letter pairs offset by 1 to 6 copies, 44% the same), so near-equal layouts differ by
copy shifts, and the search must move through them.

Periods (`placement --tandem`, against TRF's in `trtools/census.py`): chosen by coverage with letters matching one
either side of a period on, then exactly among the neighbours; the same as TRF's in 55 of the 70 `tandem` regions
and a multiple in 4; a copy layout in 61 of them.
