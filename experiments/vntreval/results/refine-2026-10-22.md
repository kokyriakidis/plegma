# The MSA refined on its sum of pairs, scored by vntr-eval (2026-10-22)

vntr-eval's 149 region packages (34 haplotypes each), every MSA turned into a graph by vntr-eval's msa_graph.py and
scored by its evaluate.py: the graph-implied pairwise cost of every haplotype pair over the pair's optimal cost,
at unit costs (`all_cost_over_opt`, the headline) and gap-affine (`affine_all_cost_over_opt`); lower is better,
1 is every pair at its own optimum. Medians per stratum, on the regions every method in the row has; better /
worse is per region against abPOA (`compare.py`).

## Where plegma lost

With abPOA's order (longest first, `-L`) plegma's POA ties abPOA (hotspot VNTRs 1.0783 vs 1.0830, 12 better, 12
worse): its earlier losses, with `-p -R 1` and `-S`, were the order. `pairs.py` on the POA's MSAs: 86-100% of the
excess over the optima lies inside the repeat cores, and 4% of haplotype pairs are at their optimum; pairs whose
lengths differ by at most 10 bp come out 1.68 times theirs. In a tandem array many alignments to the graph score
alike, and two haplotypes the same but for a letter land in different copies.

## The refinement (`-I passes,band,gap`, plegma/refine.mojo)

Each distinct sequence in turn is realigned, exactly, to the columns of the others: its letter against a column
costs the others holding another symbol there, its gap the others holding a letter, a column of its own every
other; each gap a pair newly opens costs `gap` more. A pass never raises that objective (a new row is kept only if
it costs no more), and passes go on until one gains nothing. The DP's band is centred where the sequences' optimal
pairwise alignments (karan, two-piece gap costs) place each letter: a letter is anchored to the column most of
the others vote for through their alignment to it, and the anchors kept rise in both letter and column.

| on three hotspots (sum of pairs over the optima) | |
| :-- | --: |
| POA longest first (`-L`) | 1.2247 |
| refined, band 64 around the present place | 1.1329 |
| band 64, widened while the path touches its edge | 1.1045 |
| band 256 / 1,024 | 1.0799 / 1.0786 |
| band 64 centred on the anchors | **1.0779** |

At unit costs only (`gap` 0), the refinement beat every method vntr-eval has scored on the hotspot VNTRs (1.0409,
the unit-aware realigner 1.0476) but split gaps freely, as unit costs allow: a copy of a repeat unit missing from
one haplotype became many small gaps among near-identical copies, and the affine score fell far behind (hotspot
VNTRs 1.229, non-TR SV controls 1.546). Tracing one pair showed why (L009658, `one_pair.py`): its optimum puts 8.8
missing units in one block, the MSA spread them over the array. Unit-cost optima are that ambiguous in arrays: in
hotspots 44-51% of chained pairings agree across triples of haplotypes (`consistency.py`), 54-61% with two-piece
costs.

## What did not help

- A graph rebuilt from a backbone (`-R 1`), the heaviest path or the polished consensus (`-B`): hotspot VNTRs
  1.0783 to 1.1050 and 1.1036.
- Each sequence fused along its nearest one's path (a minimum spanning tree of edit distances): 1.2667 against the
  POA's 1.2247 on three hotspots; removed.
- Columns all the pairs agree on, recursing into the windows between them (`agree.py`): they lie only in the
  flanks (inside arrays copy numbers differ, so hardly any column holds every haplotype), 1.0787, the refinement's
  own. Columns agreed by most, from the medoid's pairwise alignments pruned to agreement (`scaffold_census.py`),
  exist inside arrays (300-3,400 a region), but an MSA started from them and refined came to 1.1047.

## Open

The refinement finds a local optimum, so its start matters: on L003872 (a matched control) the longest-first POA
leaves it at 1.334 / 1.543, while from `-p` or abPOA's MSA it reaches 1.12 / 1.03, better than abPOA's 1.14 /
1.05. Refining from several starts and keeping the lowest objective (`pick_best.py`) is being measured.
