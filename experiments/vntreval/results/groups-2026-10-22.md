# Where plegma lost to the unit-aware realigner, and the two fixes (2026-10-22)

vntr-eval's 149 regions; cost over the pairwise optima (lower is better), unit / affine.

## The trace

Of the regions where unit_aware beat plegma's progressive placement (`progMSo20all`), most were trade-offs (better
unit cost, worse affine); 13 were losses on both. On every one of those, unit_aware's MSA was lower than ours on
**our own** objective (unit costs plus 1 per gap opened, `sp_score.py`), and our refinement started from its MSA
found almost nothing (gains of 0 to 113 out of 1,400 to 560,000): its MSA sat in a better local optimum that our
moves could not reach. L003672 (1.299 unit against 1.078) was no bug: our MSA was affine-optimal for every pair
(1.000 against 1.381).

Two causes, separated by refining our MSAs with each fix alone (`placement --refine`):

| region | unit_aware | ours | wide band alone | + group moves |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| L013244 | 438,315 | 453,407 | 442,353 | 442,353 |
| L011138 | 1,870,209 | 1,954,308 | 1,878,884 | 1,878,884 |
| L014210 | 121,398 | 125,647 | 125,647 | 121,799 |
| L017325 | 238,456 | 240,882 | 240,693 | 235,507 |
| L014324 | 4,619 | 5,394 | 5,394 | 4,619 |

- **Band**: the progressive harness refined with a max band of 512; long insertions need more (4096 fixes
  L013244, L011138, TR424743).
- **Moves**: one row at a time cannot shift a stretch of copies held by several haplotypes; `refine`'s group moves
  (`group_band`) realign every split of a UPGMA tree over the rows, one side's profile against the other's (exact at
  unit costs, gap opens approximated, kept only when the exact objective falls).

## Tandem-repeat tools (experiments/trtools)

On L014210 (27 bp unit) and L013244 (30 bp unit; vntr-eval annotates its 179 bp higher-order unit), every
distinct haplotype: TRF found the period and consensus in all 30, its copy counts matching vamos's; vamos decomposed
28 of 30 into its catalogue motifs (lost the 19- and 636-copy haplotypes at mapping); ULTRA gave period 60 with an
all-C consensus on L013244; longdust's intervals matched TRF's arrays within a few letters; sdust and tantan
fragmented the arrays.

`census.py` (longdust's array, TRF's smallest period covering it) over all 149: `tandem` 70 regions (one period
across haplotypes), `drifting` 40, `weak` 17, `none` 22. Median unit cost per class, unit_aware against ours:
drifting 1.0503 / 1.0410, tandem 1.0263 / 1.0266, weak 1.0211 / 1.0211. A copy-level stage belongs on the `tandem`
class alone.
